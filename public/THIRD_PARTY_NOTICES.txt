VIDEO FOOTAGE
=============

The pinned onboard MP4 is an edit of Formula 1's official George Russell
Montreal pole-lap upload:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fAQe0i2rzKg

The three landing reels are edits of publicly posted Formula 1, Mercedes, Sky
Sports, and TikTok footage. Local source metadata identifies TikTok clip IDs
7657003703106342164 and 7550758049900743958; the third reel includes visible
@fastlane_culture and Sky Sports attribution.

OPENF1 HISTORICAL DATA
======================

The golden replay data was obtained from OpenF1:
https://openf1.org/

OpenF1 publishes its project and data under Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0):
https://github.com/br-g/openf1/blob/main/LICENSE
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes: The source session was limited to George Russell, driver 63, lap 22
of 2024 Canadian Grand Prix qualifying; timestamps were normalized to the lap,
sample streams were clipped and joined, and the result was validated for
offline replay. The replay derivative is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
